# 7'x6' Forkable Dual-Purpose Hay Feeder: Build Guide

**Footprint:** 7' Wide × 6' Deep
**Features:** Built-in fork pockets for 48" loader forks, double-swing front doors for round bale access, and overhead hanging points for 3 square-bale nets.

## Sourcing Strategy: Sawmill vs. Retail
* **Buy Retail Pressure-Treated (UC4A/UC4B Ground Contact):** Sourced from a local building supply for anything in constant direct contact with soil, manure, and mud (Skids, lower frame).
* **Buy Rough-Cut from Local PA Sawmill (Hemlock / Oak):** Sourced from local mills to save 40–50%. Use Hemlock for upper framing/roofing (lightweight, rot-resistant under cover) and White Oak for flooring/slats (highly chew-resistant and structural).

## Master Cut List

### Base & Floor (7' W × 6' D Footprint)
* **3 Ground Skids:** 4×6 cut to **76"** (bevel both ends at 45° to leave a 72" flat ground footprint).
* **2 Outer Rim Joists (Front/Back):** 2×6 cut to **84"** (7'0").
* **2 Outer Side Joists (Left/Right):** 2×6 cut to **69"** (72" total minus 3" for front/back rims).
* **3 Internal Cross Joists:** 2×6 cut to **69"**.
* **4 Fork Pocket Internal Ribs:** 2×6 cut to **69"** (forms the two 7" wide boxed channels).
* **9 Floor Planks:** 2×8 (or rough 2") cut to **84"**.

### Upright Posts & Knee Bracing
* **2 Front Corner Posts:** 4×4 cut to **114"** (9'6").
* **2 Rear Corner Posts:** 4×4 cut to **96"** (8'0").
* **8 Corner Knee Braces:** 2×4 cut to **22"** with opposing 45° miter cuts on each end.

### Roof Structure
* **2 Roof Headers (Front & Back):** 2×6 cut to **96"** (leaves 6" overhang past posts on left/right).
* **5 Rafters:** 2×4 cut to **96"** (provides 12" overhang front and back past the 72" box).
* **4 Purlins (Roof Strapping):** 2×4 (or 1×4) cut to **100"** (provides 2" overhang past outer rafters).
* **3 Metal Roofing Panels:** Standard 8' length (no cut needed).

### Crib Walls & Front Split Doors
* **Left & Right Walls:** (8) 1×6 cut to **69"** (4 per side).
* **Rear Wall:** (4) 1×6 cut to **84"**.
* **Front Split Doors (2 doors total):**
  * (8) Horizontal Slats: 1×6 cut to **39"**.
  * (4) Vertical Stiles: 2×4 cut to **36"**.
  * (2) Diagonal Braces: 2×4 cut to fit diagonally across the 39" × 36" frame.

## Step-by-Step Assembly Instructions

### Step 1: Subfloor & Fork Pocket Integration
1. **Set the Skids:** Lay the three 4×6 skids parallel on flat ground: one on the left outer edge, one dead-center (at 42"), and one on the right outer edge.
2. **Build the Rim Box:** Fasten the front and rear 84" 2×6 rim joists to the side 69" joists using two 5/16" × 5" structural screws per corner. Fasten this perimeter box directly onto the 4×6 skids.
3. **Frame the Fork Pockets (Front-to-Back):**
   * Establish the center of the 84" frame (42").
   * Measure out 14" to the left and 14" to the right to locate the centerlines of your fork channels (creating a **28" on-center spacing**).
   * Frame each pocket to be **7" wide inside clearance** using parallel 69" 2×6 joists running front-to-back.
   * Frame the remaining floor area with intermediate 2×6 joists spaced ~12" to 14" on-center.
4. **Anchor Fork Retention Plates:** Underneath the frame, span the 7" fork pocket gaps by bolting the 3/16" steel plates (or 2×6 pressure-treated blocking) directly to the underside of the pocket joists at the front and at the 48" mark. This creates a fully boxed sleeve so fork tips cannot punch upward into the floorboards when lifted.

### Step 2: Lay the Heavy Deck
1. Place the 84" long rough-cut 2" oak (or 2×8 PT) boards tight together across the joists from left to right.
2. Fasten the decking using two 3" exterior screws into every joist intersection. 
3. Notch the four corner boards 3.5" × 3.5" to seat the vertical 4×4 posts directly onto the skids/frame.

### Step 3: Erect Corner Posts & Anti-Racking Braces
1. Stand the two **114" posts in front** and the two **96" posts in back**. 
2. Drill through the posts and rim joists/skids, securing each post with two 1/2" × 8" galvanized carriage bolts (smooth heads facing outside).
3. Plumb the posts with a level and install the 22" 2×4 diagonal knee braces at each top corner using structural timber screws.

### Step 4: Roof Framing & Sheeting
1. Secure the front 96" (2×6) header across the front posts at 9'6" height, and the rear 96" header across the rear posts at 8'0" height.
2. Set the five 96" 2×4 rafters on edge running front to back (spaced ~21" apart). Secure them to the headers with structural screws or hurricane ties.
3. Lay the four 100" 2×4 purlins flat across the rafters (spaced 24" apart) and screw them down.
4. Fasten the three 8' ribbed steel panels to the purlins using rubber-washer roofing screws driven through the panel ribs (not the valleys).

### Step 5: Side Walls, Rear Wall & Double Front Doors
1. **Sides and Rear:** Fasten the 1×6 horizontal slats to the exterior of the 4×4 posts using two 1-5/8" screws per post. Leave an **8" gap** between slats.
2. **Build the Front Doors:** 
   * Assemble two identical 39" wide × 36" tall door frames using the 2×4 stiles, horizontal 1×6 slats, and a diagonal 2×4 compression brace running from the bottom hinge side to the top center latch side.
   * Mount each door to the front posts using two 8" heavy-duty T-hinges per side.
   * Install the drop rod/cane bolt on the interior of one door into a pre-drilled hole in the front floor frame, and mount the heavy slide latch across both doors on the exterior.

### Step 6: Hay Net Hardware Installation
1. Drill through the center of the left, rear, and right 2×6 roof headers.
2. Install two 3/8" forged eye bolts per side, backed with heavy fender washers and double-locked nuts.
3. Clip your locking carabiners into the rings. When hanging slow-feed nets, verify that the bottom of the nets hangs at least 14" above the floorboards.
